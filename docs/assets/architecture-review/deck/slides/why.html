Targets over the first 20 reviewed PRs, against the owner’s last 20 merged PRs at work (PRD) · 2

The product PRD sets three targets over the first 20 reviewed pull requests: half the human-reviewer comments that point to a bug or an unmet requirement, at least 60% of findings followed by a code change at the finding’s location, and 30% fewer changes-requested rounds. The kill criterion: after 20 PRs, stop or rescope if useful findings are below 40% or more than half of the reviews end with incomplete coverage. Baselines are still to be collected from the owner’s last 20 merged PRs at work.
