FindingValidator, VerdictValidator, InjectionTripwire — plain C# with unit tests · 12

Validation runs without the model. A finding that fails a check is dropped and counted by reason in the summary. Requirement quotes are whitespace-normalized and matched ordinally, so a paraphrase or translation fails. The verdict keeps a criterion row only when its quote is verbatim requirement text; “yes” becomes “partly” when no row survives, when any row was dropped, or when a row or a blocking requirement mismatch contradicts it. The injection tripwire scans the requirement and the added lines for English and Portuguese phrases addressed to an AI reviewer, lists up to 10 hits, and any hit removes “good to merge” from the summary. The context hash, SHA-256 over the head SHA, the patches sent, the requirement and every tool chunk, ties each result to exactly what the model saw.
